AI Blueprint Analysis

Upload Plans. Get a Material Takeoff.

Drop a blueprint PDF and AI identifies every electrical component — panels, circuits, outlets, switches, fixtures, conduit runs, wire gauges. Materials are mapped to your catalog with quantities and labor hours auto-calculated.

  • Detects panels, circuits, outlets, switches, fixtures, conduit, transformers
  • Auto-calculates wire footage with waste factor applied
  • Distinguishes E, A, M, P sheets automatically
  • Visual overlay showing detections color-coded by system
  • Low-confidence items flagged for owner review
