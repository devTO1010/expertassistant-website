Job & Appointment Text Messages

ExpertAssistant, a product of TekOmni LLC · Last updated: November 12, 2026

ExpertAssistant job notifications are operational text messages sent by ExpertAssistant, a product of TekOmni LLC, about jobs and appointments with the electrical contractor you work for or hired. ExpertAssistant is the sender of every message and collects every opt-in itself, on its own screens; contractors never collect or give consent for anyone. Every message starts with “ExpertAssistant:” and names the contractor. They are never marketing. You receive them only if you opt in, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP.

1. Who receives texts, and what they say

Technicians and office staff of a contractor

  • A text when a job visit is assigned to you, moved, or cancelled
  • A reminder the working day before a visit
  • A reminder about an open inspection task assigned to you

Customers of a contractor

  • A confirmation when your appointment is booked
  • A reminder the day before your appointment
  • A notice if your appointment is moved

Every text starts with “ExpertAssistant:” and names the contractor in the message. Message frequency varies with how many visits are scheduled; a typical customer receives two or three texts per appointment.

Example messages

ExpertAssistant: Copper Line Electric scheduled you for Smith panel upgrade on Wed Nov 18, 8:00 AM, 123 Main St. Details: https://field.theexpertassistant.com/schedule Reply STOP to opt out.

ExpertAssistant: Your Copper Line Electric appointment is booked for Wed Nov 18, 8:00 AM-12:00 PM. Questions? Call (502) 555-0100. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opting in is always optional and is never a condition of employment, purchase, or using any part of ExpertAssistant. Entering a phone number does not opt you in; only ticking the consent box does, and it starts unticked. Nobody else can tick it for you.

3. How to stop texts, or get help

  • Reply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to any message. You will get one confirmation and no further texts from that number.
  • Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact TellMeMore@TekOmni.com, (502) 230-8224.
  • Technicians and staff can also untick the box in the field app at any time.
