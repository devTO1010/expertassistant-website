Privacy Policy

ExpertAssistant, a product of TekOmni LLC · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TekOmni LLC ("TekOmni," "we," "us," or "our"), operating the ExpertAssistant estimating and operations platform for contractors of every trade ("Service"), collects, uses, shares, and protects information. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to provide estimating, project, field and billing features:

  • Account & contact information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, business details and roles for the owners, office staff, project managers and field technicians who use the Service.
  • Your customers' information: the names, contact details and job or property addresses of the homeowners, builders and businesses you do work for, which you enter or import.
  • Operational data: estimates, catalog items and pricing, projects, jobs, schedules, time entries, photos, plans, documents, receipts, invoices and payments recorded in the Service.
  • Voice recordings and transcripts: when a user dictates an estimate or note in the field app.
  • Location at clock in and clock out: when a field technician clocks in or out in the field app, the device's GPS position and its accuracy are recorded on that time entry, so the company can confirm work at the job site. Location is read only at that moment, not tracked in between. If the technician blocks location access, the clock in or out still works and is recorded without a position.
  • Data from connected services: for example a QuickBooks Online company (Section 4) or a Microsoft OneDrive folder you choose to connect.
  • Usage & device data: log data, IP address, browser or device type, and interactions with the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Service: build and send estimates, run projects and jobs, schedule crews, track time and costs (including where a clock in or out took place, shown to your company's owners, office staff and project leads), and invoice your customers.
  • To turn voice dictation and scanned plans into draft estimates and line items for your review.
  • To send operational email, such as estimates, invoices, booking confirmations and reminders, on your behalf.
  • To keep connected services, such as QuickBooks Online, in sync with your account.
  • To secure, maintain, support and improve the Service, and to meet legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Service providers: vetted vendors that power the platform, under contracts that restrict their use of the data to providing services to us. These include cloud hosting and database providers, an email delivery provider, text message (SMS) delivery providers, speech-to-text providers that transcribe voice recordings, and artificial intelligence providers that read transcripts, plans and documents to draft estimate lines.
  • Your organization: data is shared among the authorized users of your company's account, according to their roles.
  • Your customers: the estimates, invoices and documents you choose to send them.
  • Connected services you authorize: for example, invoices and payments you choose to sync to your QuickBooks Online company.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, regulation or legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. QuickBooks Online Data

ExpertAssistant connects to QuickBooks Online only when an owner of your ExpertAssistant account chooses to connect it, and only to the one QuickBooks company that owner authorizes. We use QuickBooks data solely to provide the sync features you turn on. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train generalized artificial intelligence models.

Connecting

Connection uses Intuit's own sign-in and consent screen (OAuth 2.0) with the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting permission. ExpertAssistant never sees or stores your Intuit password. Connecting does not start any syncing by itself: an owner chooses, for each kind of record, whether QuickBooks or ExpertAssistant is the system of record, or whether that record is not synced at all.

Data read from QuickBooks

  • Customers and sub-customers (projects), vendors, products and services (items), and payment terms, for the records you choose to sync from QuickBooks.
  • Your chart of accounts, classes and tax codes, read-only, so that invoices and expenses are coded the way your books expect.
  • Item quantities on hand, read-only, if your company tracks inventory in QuickBooks and you turn that on.
  • Payments received against invoices that ExpertAssistant sent to QuickBooks, so those invoices show as paid.
  • Your company's QuickBooks company ID and name, to identify the connection.

Data written to QuickBooks

  • Invoices and the payments recorded against them in ExpertAssistant.
  • Job receipts and expenses, written as QuickBooks purchases.
  • Customers, vendors, items and payment terms, only for the records where you chose ExpertAssistant as the system of record.

Some of these features are released in stages, so your account may not yet offer every one of them. Every sync is recorded in a sync log that the owners of your account can review.

How the connection is protected

  • The access and refresh tokens Intuit issues for your company are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored, and are decrypted only on our servers when the Service calls QuickBooks on your behalf.
  • All traffic to and from QuickBooks is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Each connection belongs to one ExpertAssistant company account and is isolated from every other customer's data. Only owners can connect, change or disconnect it.
  • Notifications that Intuit sends us about changes in your company are accepted only when their Intuit signature verifies.

Disconnecting and deletion

  • An owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from Settings → Integrations in ExpertAssistant. Disconnecting revokes ExpertAssistant's tokens with Intuit and stops all syncing. You can also remove ExpertAssistant from the connected apps in your QuickBooks company settings.
  • Records already copied into ExpertAssistant, such as customers and invoices, remain part of your account's operational records after you disconnect, as with any other data you have entered. They are not deleted from QuickBooks either.
  • To ask us to delete data that came from QuickBooks, or your whole account, email TellMeMore@TekOmni.com. We will delete it, except for what we must keep to meet a legal obligation.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When an account is closed, we delete or anonymize its data within a reasonable period, except where the law requires us to keep it.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. These include encryption in transit, encryption of stored integration credentials, role-based access controls, and isolation of each company's data from every other company's. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and continuously improve our safeguards.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Update or correct account information through the Service or by contacting us.
  • Disconnect QuickBooks Online or other connected services at any time.
  • Request access to or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements.
  • Opt out of non-essential email by using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Text Messages (SMS)

If you opt in to job or appointment text messages (see Text Message Terms), we use your mobile number only to send those messages, and we keep a record of your consent (the number, the wording you agreed to, and when and where). Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. They are shared only with the text message delivery providers that carry the messages. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.
